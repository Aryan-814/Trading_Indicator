How to Read this Indicator

This system evaluates AAPL every 5 minutes and estimates price direction over the configured horizon (-- minutes) using a 2-stage LightGBM primary and meta-labeling architecture.

Signal Meanings

  • ▲ UP: High calibrated confidence that price will rise over the horizon.
  • ▼ DOWN: High calibrated confidence that price will fall over the horizon.
  • NO SIGNAL: The model chooses to abstain. In financial markets, abstaining on low-conviction bars is normal and essential.

When NOT to Trust the Indicator

  • During major macro releases (CPI, FOMC) or earnings announcements.
  • The opening settle (first 15 minutes of regular market hours: 09:30–09:45 ET).
  • When the market feed is marked STALE, DISCONNECTED, or experiencing unusual gaps.
  • During low-liquidity half-day holiday sessions (Thanksgiving Friday, Christmas Eve).

Honest Expectations & Disclaimer

Published research on intraday direction shows baseline accuracy near 50%. The model does not guarantee trading profits. This tool is designed strictly as an academic decision aid for paper trading. Not financial advice.
