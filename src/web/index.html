Indicator Methodology & Risk Guardrails [View Guide]

System Architecture

Evaluates AAPL every 5 minutes and estimates price direction over the configured horizon (60 minutes) using a 2-stage LightGBM primary classifier and secondary meta-labeling veto.

Signal Meanings

  • UP: High calibrated confidence that price will rise over the horizon ($m_t \ge \tau_m$).
  • DOWN: High calibrated confidence that price will fall over the horizon ($m_t \ge \tau_m$).
  • NO SIGNAL: The model chooses to abstain. In financial markets, abstaining on low-conviction bars is normal and essential.

When NOT to Trust the Indicator

  • During major macroeconomic announcements (CPI, FOMC, Non-Farm Payrolls).
  • The opening settle (first 15 minutes of regular market hours: 09:30–09:45 ET).
  • When market feed status is STALE, DISCONNECTED, or experiencing data gaps.
  • During low-liquidity half-day holiday sessions (Thanksgiving Friday, Christmas Eve).

Academic Research Disclaimer

Published empirical research on intraday direction shows baseline accuracy near 50%. The model does not guarantee trading profits. This tool is designed strictly as an academic research aid for paper trading. Not financial advice.
